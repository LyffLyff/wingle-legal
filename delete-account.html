Deleting your Wingle account

Wingle is published by [Full legal name]. You can delete your account and everything stored with it at any time, directly in the app.

In the app

  1. Open Wingle and go to your profile
  2. Swipe to the Settings panel
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm when asked — this cannot be undone

If you can't sign in

Email lyfflyfflyfflyfflyff@gmail.com from the address your account was registered with, and we will delete the account for you. We may ask a question only the account holder could answer before doing so.

Requests are handled within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your login credentials (email address and password)
  • Your profile: username, pronouns, age, favourite colour, animal, body of water, favourite words, chosen profile picture, catch phrase and theme
  • Your conversations and every word sent in them
  • Your friend requests, sent and received
  • Your entry in other users' friends lists

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Residual copies may remain in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for a limited period, and are not used for any purpose.

What stays on your device: your saved words, playlists and recent searches are stored only on your phone and were never uploaded — so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes them.
