2. What we collect

2.1 Account data

To create an account you provide an email address and a password. These are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password ourselves. Firebase assigns your account an anonymous user ID, which is what links the data below to you.

2.2 Profile data

If you create a profile, the following is stored in our database:

2.3 Social data

  • Friends list — the user IDs of people you are friends with.
  • Friend requests — sender and recipient IDs, the sender's username and profile picture, and the request status.
  • Messages — in Wingle a message is a single dictionary word. We store the word, who sent it, and when. There is no free-text chat, so a message cannot contain anything you type yourself.

2.4 Stored only on your device

The following never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the App:

  • Your saved words and playlists
  • Your recent dictionary searches
  • Your colour theme setting
  • The offline dictionary
  • Your login token, so you stay signed in

2.5 What we do not collect

Wingle contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking technologies. We do not build usage profiles, do not use advertising identifiers, and do not sell or rent personal data to anyone. We do not collect your phone number and never send you SMS messages. The App does not use cookies; the browser version stores the items listed in 2.4 locally so that it can work offline.

3. Why we process it, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the App — creating your account, showing your profile, letting you add friends and send words. Legal basis: performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • To keep the App secure — authenticating you and preventing misuse. Legal basis: legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • To meet legal obligations where applicable. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

Providing profile details beyond your email address is optional in the sense that they are only used to display your profile to friends; without an account you can still use the dictionary and save words locally.

4. Who else processes your data

We use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, as a processor on our behalf. All account, profile and social data described in section 2 is stored on Google's infrastructure.

This can involve a transfer of personal data to the United States. Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses as a safeguard for such transfers. Further information is available in Google's Firebase privacy documentation.

Beyond Google, we share your data with no one. Other users of the App see only what you would expect them to: your profile and the words you send them.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data — for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed (see section 6).
  • Messages — for as long as the conversation exists. Deleting your account deletes your conversations.
  • Friend requests — until accepted, declined, or your account is deleted.
  • Backups — residual copies may persist in our processor's encrypted backups for a limited period after deletion, and are not used for any other purpose.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  1. Open the App and go to your profile
  2. Open the Settings panel
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

This permanently deletes:

  • Your login credentials
  • Your profile and all fields listed in section 2.2
  • Your conversations and the words in them
  • Your friend requests, and your entry in your friends' lists

Words and playlists saved on your device are not affected, because we never had them — remove them by uninstalling the App.

If you can no longer sign in, email lyfflyfflyfflyfflyff@gmail.com from the address your account uses and we will delete it for you. See also our account deletion page.
